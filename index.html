Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What are your clinic hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Emergency services are available 24/7 for critical cases.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment through our online form, call us at +91 98765 43210, or visit our clinic in person. Online bookings receive instant confirmation.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept all major health insurance plans including HDFC Insurance, ICICI Lombard, Max Bupa, Aetna, and many more. Please contact us to confirm your specific plan coverage.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. Contact us via phone, email, or through your patient portal for rescheduling.

Are prescriptions available online?

Yes, after your consultation, prescriptions are sent directly to your email and can be used at any pharmacy. We also offer prescription delivery services.

Do you offer telemedicine services?

Yes, we offer telemedicine consultations for general checkups and follow-ups. Book your video consultation through our platform and get expert advice from home.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, digital wallets (Google Pay, Apple Pay), bank transfers, and UPI payments. Installment plans are also available for major procedures.

Is my medical data secure?

We use industry-standard encryption and comply with all healthcare privacy regulations including HIPAA. Your personal and medical data is completely protected and confidential.
